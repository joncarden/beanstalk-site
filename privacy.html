🌱 Beanstalk

Privacy Policy · Last updated 25 July 2026

Beanstalk collects no personal data.

Not "collects a little." None. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server operated by us that your data is sent to. This page explains what that means in detail, because a privacy policy that just says "we care about your privacy" tells you nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Beanstalk has no sign-up, no login, no email field, and no password. We never receive your name, your children's names, your balances, or your usage.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter ‚Äî kids' names and emoji, ledger entries, notes, goals, allowance and interest settings, and your Kid Mode PIN ‚Äî is stored locally on your device.

What goes to iCloud

If you have iCloud enabled, that same data syncs through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it reaches your other devices and any co-parent you invite. This is Apple's infrastructure operating on your behalf under your Apple Account. We have no access to it, no credentials for it, and no ability to read it. Apple's handling is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

If you turn iCloud off, Beanstalk still works fully ‚Äî it simply stops syncing.

Co-parent sharing

Inviting a co-parent uses Apple's CKShare. They join with their existing Apple Account. We never see the invitation, their identity, or the shared contents. The name shown beside entries ("logged by Dad") is a label you or they typed, stored in the same private iCloud data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details, no card number, and no billing address ‚Äî only Apple's signed confirmation that this device is entitled to the unlock. We cannot identify you from it.

Notifications

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server. They are off until you turn them on.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry SDKs of any kind.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party trackers.
  • No data sold, rented, shared, or brokered ‚Äî there is nothing to sell.
  • No crash-reporting service that receives your content.
  • No connection to any bank, card network, or payment processor.

Children

Beanstalk is designed for a parent to use, with a Kid Mode a child may view on the parent's device. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children. We do not knowingly receive personal information from anyone, of any age, because there is no mechanism by which we could.

Your rights

Rights such as access, portability, correction, and erasure (under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws) are normally exercised by asking a company for your data. Here, you already hold all of it:

  • Access and portability: Settings → Export produces a CSV of your complete ledger, at any time, including after the trial ends.
  • Correction: edit or undo any entry in the app.
  • Erasure: delete the app, then remove its iCloud data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. Nothing remains anywhere else.

We cannot delete your data on request for the simple reason that we do not have it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes materially, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published here before the change takes effect in a released version of the app.
